Bake Injera with Hindek &
Sip Sacred Coffee with Grandpa

True travel isn't just seeing landmarks; it is sharing bread and sitting down for a warm cup of coffee with elders. In our private kitchen class in Addis Ababa, you are welcomed not as a tourist, but as a long-awaited guest.

1

Hands-On 100% Teff Injera Baking

Learn ancient multi-day fermentation, see the 'eyes' (ayen) rise on the clay mitad, and peel warm injera with your own hands.

2

The 3-Round Grandpa Coffee Blessing

Roast raw Kafa coffee beans over charcoal, waft frankincense smoke, and taste Abol (strength), Tona (depth), and Baraka (blessing).

3

Cook seasoned beef or lamb tibs, organic lentil misir wat, and vegetarian fasting delicacies spiced with family-crafted Berbere.

Short-Term Language & Cultural Preparation
